# Handoff — hold timer, per-exercise sheet, live-workout restyle, Up Next coach note

*Updated 2026-09-17. Branch `feat/hold-timer`, HEAD `581730e6b`. **Every phase of the blueprint is
implemented and committed. Nothing is pushed.***

## What this is

A timed set (a plank, a carry, a cardio interval) had no timer: the app prescribed 45 seconds and
the only way to record them was to count them and type the number. That grew into the rest of the
blueprint — a per-exercise sheet, a restyle of the live workout and Up Next, and the paragraph the
generator writes about the session as a whole.

The blueprint is the spec and is deliberately outside the repo, matching the other blueprints:

    ~/fitness/EXERCISE-SHEET-BLUEPRINT.md

It carries a **reconciliation block per phase** recording where the plan disagreed with the code and
which of the two won. Read those before changing any of this; several of them are the reason a thing
looks the way it does (why the hold's lock-screen button is `+15s` and Stop rather than `±15s`, why
the watch complication was deliberately not built, why the web coach note does not collapse).

## Open risks

1. **The hold's Live Activity rendering is unverified.** The widget extension is regenerated by
   `npx expo prebuild --clean` and drawn by WidgetKit, neither of which is reachable from
   `pnpm run validate` or jest, and neither is available in the environment this was built in. The
   props, the labels, the interaction targets and the store wiring are all covered by tests
   (including a layout-source contract test), but the *picture* is not. After the next prebuild,
   eyeball two things on a real lock screen: the `stop.fill` / `+15s` button pair at
   `controlSize('large')`, and the countdown's 40pt cap under ten minutes.
2. **The watch-face complication in the F3 backlog line was not built, on purpose.** As of F2 the
   Live Activity's `bannerSmall` slot already puts the hold countdown in the watchOS Smart Stack,
   and a complication's timeline-reload budget cannot keep a 45-second countdown current anyway. The
   F3 reconciliation block records what building it would have cost if it is ever wanted.
3. **Three files are dirty in the working tree and are deliberately not in any of these commits** —
   they predate this work: `SparkyFitnessMobile/src/services/api/exerciseApi.ts`,
   `SparkyFitnessMobile/__tests__/services/api/exerciseApi.test.ts`, and
   `SparkyFitnessMobile/targets/watch/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/Contents.json`.
4. **The hold labels are English-only in PL and ES.** `activeWorkout.liveActivity.hold` /
   `.stopHold` exist in the EN catalog only and fall back to English, which is the documented
   contract until Weblate ships a translation;
   `__tests__/services/workoutLiveActivityLabels.test.ts` names them in one place
   (`UNTRANSLATED_HOLD_LABELS`) so the assertion is replaced rather than rediscovered.
5. **Web and mobile holds are separate implementations of one behaviour.** They share no code — the
   web keeps its countdown in the `localStorage` playback draft, mobile in the persisted Zustand
   store — so a change to how a hold logs its set has to be made twice. The F5b reconciliation block
   says why, and both floor the logged duration at one second and log what was actually held.
